<p>Beer Tracker is a homebrewed (!) app to track different ales what I have drunk. 
As it meets my personal needs, anyone can register and use it for FREE.</p>

<p>It is difficult to build a database of all the ales in the world so this app 
builds the data from an AI search. Once added to the database, it is retrieved 
from there and so we build up a fast and comprehensive encyclopedia of beer.</p>

<p>This App is currently in the initial stages of development so features will be 
added as we progress.</p>
